## Model output cascade you insist on (**OUTPUT FORMAT clause** duplicate every time)

1. **Executive plan** sentence batch (systems changing + why risky).  
2. **SPEC** — acceptance criteria cleanly split from tech noise.  
3. **Architectural schematic** noting concurrency + duplicate-processing hazards.  
4. **Operationalized steps** (owners per step).  
5. **Explicit trade-offs** contrasting lean vs hardened paths.

Whenever steps blur into stakeholder theater without falsifiable TECH spec bullets, tighten follow-ups carrying **missing slices only** (token discipline).

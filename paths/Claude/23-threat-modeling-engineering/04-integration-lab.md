Produce living artefacts marrying earlier units:

Enumerate **Assets** granularly—not generic “payments” label only—ledger rows, PSP webhooks tokens, reconciliation batch identities, ops dashboards.

Enumerate **Threats** with STRIDE tags prioritised realistically for your deployment shape—include **EoP via overly broad MCP / CI roles** where applicable.

Craft **Verification** backlog: regression security tests desired, infra policy guard clauses, tabletop timing expectations—not aspirational unicorn promises.

Measurements:

Coverage metric honesty—percentage assets touched by at least one threat row  

Residual risk items explicitly ACKed with named owner—no orphaned rows

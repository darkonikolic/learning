# Incident Commander & Production Leadership — framing

## Phase framing (`39`)

**Units:** `01`–`05`.

### Expansion beyond tactical triage

You already practise evidence ladders (`10`). Leadership layer adds:

**Incident Commander (IC)** role clarity & succession  

**Stakeholder communication** pacing (internal & external optics)  

**Status page ownership** factual minimalism discipline  

**Blameless postmortem** culture & corrective action backlog hygiene  

**Severity model** mapping symptom → SLA class → escalation  

**Oncall culture** sustainable load budgeting / shadow / handoff artefacts  

**Runbook ownership lifecycle** versioning next to **`25` rules/skills maturity**

### Success signal

Incident narrative quality improves when **outside observer** trusts timeline—not only when root cause patched.

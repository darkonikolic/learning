# Evolution thinking

**Theme:** Principals optimise for **tomorrow’s constraints**, not yesterday’s comforts.

Central question shifts from “what is it today?” to “what breaks at **order-of-magnitude** growth?”

### Illustrative stretching exercises

Symfony / Laravel tenancy: conceptual jump **100k users → millions**  

Go serving path: throughput **100 rps → 10k rps** class (hypothetical—ground in architecture, not slogan)

Enumerate:

**Growth** stresses (writes, reads, integrations, regulation)  

**Migration** eras (dual-write bridges, phased rollouts)

**Future boundary** redraws  

**Future bottlenecks** likely emerging sooner than intuition claims

Discuss **Evolution × Cost**: bigger is not cheaper—articulate marginal cost intuition.

Discuss **Evolution × Security**: larger blast radius demands stricter approval and threat models.

**LAB:** Write a two-horizon narrative (e.g. 12 months / 24 months) with **explicit invalidated assumptions** list—signals you will monitor to revise plan.

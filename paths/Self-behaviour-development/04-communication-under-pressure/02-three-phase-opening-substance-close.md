# Three-move answer shaping — optional frame · core · grounding close

## What this settles

Allow answers to adopt **opening frame → factual centre → stabilising orientation** selectively—not every interrogative demands theatrical triple scaffolding; often **middle alone suffices**.

## Story / lens

Outer frame orients politely; nucleus answers question actually asked; grounding line returns dependents / agendas / logistical north star without argumentative detouring. Oversized banks tempt cramming ornate frames blindly—sense **stripping needless jacket** consciously. Rhythm discipline borrows pacing rigour neighbouring **[golden chain](01-golden-chain-pause-structure-silence.md)**.

Sentence inventory inspiration (adapt voice): courteous opener (“To stay precise…”), middle factual vertebra, grounding orientation (“What I can commit next is…”).

## Moves

~**Twenty mixed prompts aloud** annotate mentally **needs frame? grounding tail?** pre-speech → answer accordingly (often nucleus alone).

Heavy prompt single pass: enact complete triplet ≤4 succinct cumulative sentences aloud; recorder playback judges escalation slope not lexical jewellery.

# Golden chain — pause · chunk · chosen silence

## What this settles

Shape default cadence **`stimulus → intentional gap (~≥2 seconds early phase) → 1–3 sentence cluster → purposeful stop → no filler syllables awaiting their cue`**. Silence becomes **architecture**, not indictment.

## Story / lens

Panels reward **regulated tempo**. Micro-gap purchases honest processing clocks; interviewer steering modular Q&A receives iterative chunks instead of improvised manifesto dumps. Anxiety relabel: quiet slot ≠ character failing.

## Moves

• **Mechanical pacing reps** audible neutral prompts ⇒ visible two-second widening early phase ⇒ answer capped three sentences ⇒ mouth sealed until tactile next cue ⇒ eradicate **“…and another thing”** parasite across ten arcs.

• **Dual block breathing** heavier prompts ⇒ max **two** blocks first week segments each ≤three sentences partitioned by conspicuous breath-shoulder slump ⇒ internal cap ~45s continuous speech ⇒ forced split.

• **Silence reassurance micro-line** inward whisper **“slot—not failure”** once when quiet itchy—no audible filler injection.

Calibration: ten arcs without compulsive apology-for-quiet sentence.

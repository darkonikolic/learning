# Preparing for evaluator or expert sessions

**This is not a substitute for legal counsel. Always align your preparation with your lawyer before any session.**

## What this is

When you have a session with an evaluator, expert, or similar professional in a custody or safeguarding process, what you say matters — but so does how you say it. Evaluators notice whether you are calm and clear, whether you keep the focus on your child, and whether you can describe conflict without attacking the other parent.

This file helps you prepare what to cover and how to rehearse it.

## How to prepare

**Five topic areas to prepare:**

1. **Your personal background** — a brief, factual description of who you are. Keep it under 25 seconds. Lead with stable caregiving, not with achievements or grievances.

2. **Description of the conflict** — describe what happened without attacking the other party. Keep the child in the foreground. Evaluators notice when a parent cannot describe conflict without it becoming an attack.

3. **Your parenting picture** — your child's daily routines, their interests, your relationship with them, how you spend time together. This should be specific and concrete.

4. **How you handle stress** — be honest and practical. What do you do when things are hard? What support do you have?

5. **Logistics** — practical arrangements, schedules, how you handle transitions and communication.

**For each topic:**
Mark your confidence as high, medium, or low. Any topic marked low gets one recorded aloud practice pass before the session.

## How to rehearse

**Practice aloud, not in your head.**
Reading notes silently does not prepare you for speaking under pressure. Say the answers out loud. Record yourself if possible and listen back.

**Crisis or difficult questions:**
Practice answering hard questions in no more than two clear sentences. Stop after two sentences. Do not over-explain.

**Dry-run standard:**
Keep rehearsing until you can do three consecutive self-reviews where your speed and volume stay steady throughout. That is when you are ready.

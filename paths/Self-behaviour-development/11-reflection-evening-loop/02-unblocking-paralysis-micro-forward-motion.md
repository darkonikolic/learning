# Unblocking — dread typing and micro forward motion

## What this settles

Separate **learned dread** from **evidence-backed threat**; when frozen, commit the smallest honest next executable step; keep crisis language from turning every fear into courtroom theatre.

## Story / lens

Learned fear borrows catastrophic imagery from older loops; **realistic** hazards belong on **professional routes**, not solo rhetorical marathons. When planning collapses into infinite research, compress to **one truthful next physical or administrative move**. Pair physiological spin with **[regulation somatic](../02-regulation-and-recovery/)** work **before** you argue yourself in circles.

## Moves

List ten anxieties; mark **L** (learned spiral) versus **R** (material hazard)—**R** items need external help, not self-debate alone.

Blocked more than ~30 minutes on a task → **five-minute** honest entry only—timer honoured; log done versus stopped.

Mind loops absolutes → return to **[somatic resets](../02-regulation-and-recovery/)**, then cognition.

During a declared block week, ship **at least one** forward micro daily.

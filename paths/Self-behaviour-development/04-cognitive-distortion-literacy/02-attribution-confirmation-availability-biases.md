# Attribution, confirmation, availability — justice feelings vs evidence

## What this settles

Spot how stress tilts **how you assign blame** (**fundamental attribution error**—their character vs your circumstances), **how you hunt facts** (**confirmation bias**—noticing what fits the verdict), and **how vivid examples hijack probability** (**availability bias**—last ugly message feels like universal law).

Layer **projection awareness** here when you attribute motives you fear in yourself. Keep each tag short; the move is earlier correction, not academic taxonomy.

## Story / lens

Under load, memory serves **coherence** (the story already installed) more than **completeness**. That makes peaceful channels look fake and hostile ones look “obvious.” Slowing one notch protects **[channel discipline](../07-audiences-channels-boundaries/)** and keeps paperwork aligned with what you can actually show.

## Moves

For three fresh frictions, write two lines: **Internal headline (justice version)** vs **Witness-only caption (camera version).** If the gap is huge, park the headline before sending.

**Disconfirm search:** one sentence of evidence *against* your favourite theory—must be honest; if none exists yet, label *unknown.*

**Motivation hypotheses:** downgrade “they did it because evil” → “possible drivers include ___, ___—I haven’t verified which.” Stop at plausible plural unless procedure demands proof.

Stress-volume check: revisit after **[sleep/load sheet](../02-regulation-and-recovery/03-sleep-protection-fatigue-and-recovery-signals.md)** whenever bias labels spike at night—the same quarrel reads different when depleted.

# Tolerance window, reset ladders, truthful delay wording, anchors

## What this settles

Recognise drifting **outside functional nervous-system bandwidth** sooner; intervene with physiology + containment labelling inside ~30 seconds; deploy **truthful postpone lines** ethically; anchors only after deliberate pairing groundwork.

## Story / lens

“Off-window” isn’t melodrama wording—it resembles executive narrowing: looping absolutist thoughts, cervical lock voice, argumentative magnetism irresistible. Buying time honours integrity when timelines promised are **real** (“I’ll revert after verifying documents”) — invented deadlines poison institutional trust. Anchors become optional amplifiers **only after** serene pairing nights; gimmick substitutes absent repetition mislead nobody including yourself.

## Moves

• **Recognition deck** — jot six unmistakable tells; shuffle verbally naming tell + obligatory first manoeuvre (**exhale + feet-floor feel alone**, never instant debate).

• **Reset ladder repetitions (~5 arcs)** resurrect last conflict memory or surrogate role play: inward tag (**role + procedural container—not entire biography**) ⇒ shoulder-drop exhale ⇒ **singular** micro-move (muted sentence vs dignified silence vs honest defer phrase).

Timer ≤30 seconds total.

• **Truthful postpone inventory** forge **two phrases** calibrated per arena (employment / co-parenting / clinician) spoken aloud calmly ×10 rotations each—discard anything promising schedules you intend to fracture.

• **Anchor clause** practise calm pairing bedtime micro ≥5 successive nights premised before courtroom deployment else omit strata entirely.

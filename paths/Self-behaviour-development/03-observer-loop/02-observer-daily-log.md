# Observer daily log — channel inventory, not self-prosecution

> After any contact that activated your system — difficult conversation, written exchange, or internal pressure spike — fill this within 30 minutes. Sooner than that: too hot. Later than 30 min: details blur.

---

## The three channels

You observe these simultaneously. The goal is to name them — not fuse into the story.

| Channel | What it tracks | Example signal |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Physical sensations, tension, breath changes | Chest tightening, jaw clenching, faster breathing, stillness |
| Impulse | Urge to act before thinking | Want to reply now, want to leave, want to prove, want to snipe |
| Story | The narrative your mind generates | "They're trying to corner me", "I'm failing", "This means..." |

---

## Post-contact entry (fill within 30 min)

```
Date / time:        ________
Contact type:       [ ] In-person  [ ] Written  [ ] Phone  [ ] Internal pressure (no contact)
Context (1 line):   ________

BODY — what did I notice?
  ________

IMPULSE — what did I want to do in the moment?
  ________

STORY — what narrative ran first?
  ________

Did I act from body/impulse, or did I observe first?
  [ ] Observed first — paused, then responded
  [ ] Acted from impulse — response came before observation
  [ ] Mixed — partly observed, partly reactive

What did I actually do?
  ________

One token label:
  [ ] Observer-stable — noticed without fusing
  [ ] Impulse — acted from urgency
  [ ] Slipped on: ________  (name the specific distortion or pattern)
```

## Worked example

```
Date / time:        2026-03-14  18:45
Contact type:       [x] Written — email from opposing party
Context:            Message questioned my parenting decision. Implied negligence.

BODY:        Chest tightened. Warm face. Sat forward toward screen.
IMPULSE:     Wanted to reply immediately with three paragraphs defending myself.
STORY:       "They're building a case against me. This will be used in court."

Did I act from body/impulse?
  [x] Mixed — paused for 2 minutes, then drafted reply. Sent after 20 min review.

What I did:
  Waited 20 minutes, reread message, classified it as [P = Provocation] per situation
  literacy. Replied with two factual sentences only.

Token: Observer-stable — classified first, replied second.
```

---

## Weekly pattern scan

Once a week, look at your log entries. Count:

```
Observer-stable entries:   ___
Impulse entries:           ___
Slipped on [___]:          ___ (note which pattern appears most)
```

If one pattern appears 3+ times in a week — it goes into the drill schedule for that scenario type.

---

## Integration

Impulse entries feed into [Six routing cards drill](../05-communication-under-pressure/03-six-techniques-and-negation-protocol.md) — practice the specific scenario type that triggered the impulse.

Slipped-on patterns feed into [Cognitive distortion literacy](../04-cognitive-distortion-literacy/) — verify which distortion was running.

Stable entries matter too — note which skill moved (pause, technique routing, breath anchor) so you can deliberately repeat it.

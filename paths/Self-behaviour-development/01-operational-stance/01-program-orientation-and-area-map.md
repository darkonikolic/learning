# How this learning path works

## What this is

This path teaches practical behavioural skills for high-pressure situations — conversations, conflicts, difficult relationships, institutional settings. The skills cover everything from staying calm in the moment to understanding your own thinking patterns to managing your physical state under stress.

The files are short on purpose. The value comes from practising the moves, not from reading more pages.

## How to use it

You do not need to go in order. Use the self-check below to find where you are leaking most right now — then open that folder.

**Self-check — read each line and notice which feels weakest:**

- I take a breath before responding when something hits me
- I say one thing, stop, and wait — instead of stacking arguments
- I stay calm in my body before my mind tries to win an argument
- I only engage with one tactic at a time, not multiple at once
- I keep my emotional state contained when it matters

Whichever row feels consistently weak — that is where you start.

**Three-question check (takes 60 seconds):**

1. Who is the most high-stakes person or situation in my life right now? _____
2. Which row above disappears most often under pressure? _____
3. Which folder number should I open next (see map below)? _____

# Program orientation — how these folders relate

## What this settles

High-stakes contexts reward **steady behaviour**, not panic-forged eloquence. Sheets stay short **on purpose**; depth arrives from living the moves, not stacking pages. Open folders **by friction** (`01`–`13`): whichever competency leaks tomorrow is tomorrow’s chapter—there is no secret mandatory sequence.

Nothing here replaces therapy or counsel. Where custody-shaped language appears, treat it as **communication literacy only**—align contested statements with whoever holds your legal brief.

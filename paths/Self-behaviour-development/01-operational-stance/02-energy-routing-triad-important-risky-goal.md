# Energy routing — important · risky · does a real goal exist

## What this settles

Train a sub-five-second calculus: **Does this messenger matter to my actual responsibilities? carries asymmetric risk? attaches to a workable aim?** — then choke bandwidth consciously instead of debating yourself into burnout.

## Story / lens

Few pings merit equal arousal budgets. Neither importance-only nor justice feelings justify feeding bottomless arenas. Important-without-payoff-but-high-risk ⇒ shift to transactional tone, textual trace, or delegated escalation — not live duels meant for counsel / HR equivalents. Labels **Important · Risk · Goal** dovetail broader sequencing in **[Conflict ladders](../12-active-life-design/02-conflict-decision-sequence-flag-pointers.md)** and FAST routing **[here](03-fast-playbook.md)**.

## Moves

1. **Mental sting sets** (~10 cues) — imagine precise hooks (traffic blame ping, petty workplace dig, parental guilt lure). Vocalise exclusively `Imp=yes/no · Risk=yes/no · Goal=yes/no/absent`. If importance & aim both evaporate ⇒ **minimal or silent plan**. Pace target <5 seconds; zero fantasy closing argument.

2. **One starvation corridor weekly** — name an attention sink (human, feed, chatter group) yielding negative ROI; document the guardrail once; annotate one lapse without moral tribunal.

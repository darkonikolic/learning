# Message classification drill — I · P · H routing practice

> Practice before the real messages arrive. 30 lines, solo, 15–20 minutes. Goal: classification reflex under 2 seconds.

---

## Classification system

| Tag | Stands for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Inform | Genuine request for factual information or logistics |
| **P** | Provoke | Designed to trigger emotional response — correct answer is no insult mirror |
| **H** | Hook | Invites you into a narrative or story frame you didn't author and shouldn't defend |

---

## Drill procedure

**Step 1.** Read each line below. Do not answer it. Assign only I / P / H.

**Step 2.** After tagging all 30, go back and for P and H items: pick one routing card (1–6) per item.

**Step 3.** For your toughest 5–8 items: speak the tag aloud + routing digit within 2 seconds of re-reading.

---

## Routing card reference (from Six routing cards)

| # | Use when... |
|---|---|
| 1 | Narrowly scoped factual — answer directly, briefly |
| 2 | Redirect to child/care-forward without psychoanalyzing absent parties |
| 3 | Precision — tighten a vague or bombardment question |
| 4 | Partial witness — you can confirm a fact, but not the full accusation |
| 5 | Channel redirect — this belongs to a different person or process |
| 6 | Refuse the premise — the question assumes something you do not accept |

---

## Drill set A — low to medium intensity

Classify each (I / P / H) and, for P and H, add routing digit.

1. "What time does pick-up happen on Saturday?"
2. "You always make everything about yourself."
3. "Everyone can see what kind of person you really are."
4. "Did you confirm the appointment with the doctor?"
5. "Why do you think the children are happy with you?"
6. "Can you send me the school schedule for next month?"
7. "After everything I've done, this is how you behave."
8. "What does your therapist say about your anger issues?"
9. "I need the insurance documents by end of week."
10. "You were always the difficult one in the relationship."

---

## Drill set B — medium to high intensity

11. "Have you explained to the children why we are in this situation?"
12. "Don't you think the children deserve a stable parent?"
13. "I'm just asking a simple question — why can't you answer it?"
14. "What time will you be back with the children on Sunday?"
15. "Your behavior in the last meeting was embarrassing for everyone."
16. "Is it true that you told the children X?" (where X is false)
17. "How much do you earn per month currently?"
18. "My parents are worried about the children's wellbeing."
19. "Why do you think you're a better parent than I am?"
20. "I need written confirmation of the arrangement we discussed."

---

## Drill set C — sustained pressure and combined patterns

21. "You did X, then Y, then Z — don't you see the pattern?" (multiple accusations stacked)
22. "The children told me what happened. I think we both know the truth."
23. "Can we just talk like adults for once?"
24. "What's your plan for the summer holidays?"
25. "Everyone who knows you says the same thing."
26. "I'm not angry. I just want to understand why you chose to do that."
27. "Don't you feel guilty?"
28. "What are your thoughts on joint custody going forward?"
29. "You know this is going to come up in the proceedings."
30. "I'm doing this for the children. I hope you can say the same."

## Weekly rotation rule

Do not drill the same set twice in a row. Rotate: A → B → C → A.

After each session: log one line — which item was hardest and why.

> Pattern: if the same item type appears hard repeatedly → it identifies the scenario cluster to rehearse in [Integration layer](../10-drills-and-exercise-archive/01-integration-layer-scenario-loops.md).

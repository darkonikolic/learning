# Two registers — private candour versus outward humane clarity

## What this settles

Keep **inside register** candid enough for strategising martyrdom away; outward register stays humane, factual, responsibly scoped—preventing hostility exports that immortalise catastrophically in writing.

## Story / lens

Translating wrath into operational clarity differs from censorship: goal is halt **dual damage** cascade (self-worth collapse + dependents reading poison forever). Trusted channels (therapy paper, shredding journal voice) harbour raw verbs; transactional / institutional / offspring-facing corridors receive cooler nouns—not fake sweetness, workable precision.

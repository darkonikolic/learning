# Structuring a complex answer — opening, substance, close

## What this is

Some answers need a little more shape than just a direct reply. For those, you can use a simple three-part structure: a brief orienting phrase that sets up what you are about to say, the actual answer, and a grounding close that points forward rather than backward.

The important thing is to know when NOT to use it. Most of the time, the answer alone is enough. Forcing this structure onto every response makes you sound rehearsed and unnatural. Use it when the question is complex and a bare answer would leave things unclear.

## When to use this

- When a question requires a longer answer and you want to stay organised
- When you need to give a nuanced response without wandering
- When you want to end on something concrete rather than leaving things open
- Do NOT use this for simple, direct questions — just answer those directly

## How to do it

**Part 1 — Opening phrase (optional):**
One short sentence that orients the listener before you go into your answer. Its job is to signal what kind of answer is coming, not to pad the response.
Examples: "To be precise about what happened..." or "There are two parts to this..."

**Part 2 — The actual answer:**
This is the core. State the facts, your position, or what you know. Keep it tight. This is the part that matters.

**Part 3 — Grounding close (optional):**
End with something that looks forward or anchors the conversation. This prevents the answer from trailing off into vagueness.
Examples: "What I can commit to from here is..." or "The next step on my end is..."

**Check before you speak:**
Ask yourself: does this answer actually need an opening phrase? Does it need a closing line? If not, drop them. The answer alone is usually enough.

## Example

Question: "What's your plan for handling communication going forward?"

Without structure (fine for a simple version): "I'm keeping exchanges brief and in writing."

With structure (when more context is needed): "To be clear about what I'm changing — I'm keeping all communication in writing and limiting it to logistics. What I can commit to is responding within 24 hours on anything related to the kids."

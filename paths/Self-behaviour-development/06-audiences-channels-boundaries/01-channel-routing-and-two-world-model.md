# Channel routing — which truth belongs where

## What this settles

Keep **live comportment / owned stable facts** cleanly separated from **paper trail / counsel / mediated writing** channels—mixing improvisational boasts with evidentiary lanes produces self-inflicted wounds.

## Story / lens

Evaluative worlds parse **oral affect** distinct from **binder contents**; knowledge-work analogues echo **Slack heat vs ticket coolness** divergence. Emotional bait arriving live still answers better as **flattened administrative noun statements** than improvised trial closings. Sensitive phrasing meaningfully legal belongs with counsel review — this sheet names **routing buckets**, not substitutes counsel voice.

## Moves

Sketch **quadrant** on paper per live conflict cluster: **Witness-visible behaviour** • **Short oral facts I own cleanly** • **Delegate to advisor/dossier** • **Forbidden improvisational story branches** — fill tranquilly offstage.

~**eight blended bait lines** read neutrally aloud — you answer first by **vocal channel label** (Arena A live demeanour vs Arena B written/counsel) stub before elaborated safe content.

Constraint: **never** rebuild full evidentiary architecture from fragile boast-memory when uncertain — anchor unknowns to advisement.

# Expert / safeguarding-style scenario prep — execution lab

**Legal alignment with counsel remains mandatory—not a substitute for it.**

## What this settles

Cover the **topic map**, rehearse a **compact brief skeleton**, maintain **prep tone**: steady volume, chronological clarity, resisting prosecutorial tangents at absent third parties—when counsel approves the plan.

## Story / lens

Compress what evaluators notice before substantive questions arrive—biography without overshare, restrained conflict description that keeps children forward, pragmatic translation of tests without jargon shields, contingency language when sleep or logistics crater. Tie tempo discipline to neighbouring **[communication under pressure](../04-communication-under-pressure/)** units.

**Doorway autobiography:** aim **under ~25 seconds** so dependable caregiving arcs lead, not applause arcs. Invite counsel critique whenever stakes justify it.

## Moves

Print five topical headers (**biography, conflict/process, evaluations/tests, direct observation cues, child’s daily picture, logistical execution**)—mark confidence high/med/low; anything low earns one recorded aloud pass.

Swap placeholder biography lines for a truthful factual arc, record audio, shave filler—closing thrust favours stability trajectory where context fits counsel guidance.

Deliver three index-card crises aloud—maximum two declarative sentences, mandatory stop afterward; coach flags volume creep.

Dry-run until three consecutive video self-reviews stay free of speed and volume creep.

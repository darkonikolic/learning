# Calm your body before you speak

## What this is

When you are stressed, angry, or anxious, your body reacts before your mind catches up. If you speak or type from that activated state, what comes out is driven by the stress — not by clear thinking.

This file gives you two small habits:

1. **A physical reset before speaking** — feel your feet, relax your jaw, breathe out slowly, then speak or type.
2. **Name the emotion to yourself** — when you feel irritated or upset, say one word internally to name it before deciding whether to respond.

Neither of these is visible to anyone else. Both take under ten seconds.

## When to use this

- Before responding to any message that activated you
- Before speaking in a difficult conversation
- When you notice your jaw is tight, your chest is tense, or you are holding your breath
- When irritation spikes and you feel the urge to say something immediately

## How to do it

**Physical reset (before speaking or typing):**

1. Feel your feet on the floor — notice the contact, even briefly
2. Check your jaw — if it is tight, let it go slightly
3. Breathe out slowly, longer than the breath in
4. Now speak or type

That is the whole thing. It takes about five seconds. The point is not relaxation — it is that these three physical actions interrupt the automatic chain from stress to reaction.

**Naming the emotion (when irritated alone or before a reply):**

Say one word to yourself internally. Just one: "anger", "fear", "pressure", "hurt". Not a sentence. Not an explanation. Not a story about why.

After naming it, ask: do I respond now, or do I wait and come back to this?

This is not about suppressing the emotion. It is about creating a half-second gap between the feeling and the action.

## Example

You read a message that feels unfair and accusatory. Your first impulse is to defend yourself immediately.

Instead: feet on the floor, jaw check, slow breath out. Then say to yourself: "anger". Now decide — respond now or in 20 minutes? Most of the time, the answer is 20 minutes.

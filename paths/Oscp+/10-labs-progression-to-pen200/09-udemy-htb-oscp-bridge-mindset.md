# Supplementary Video Courses for OSCP Prep

Video courses supplement lab practice — they don't replace it. Lab hours matter more than video hours.

## Recommended Courses

**TCM Security — Practical Ethical Hacking**
- URL: tcm-sec.com
- Cost: ~$30
- Covers: full methodology end-to-end, Active Directory attacks, report writing
- Best for: filling methodology gaps before starting HTB/PG

**TCM Security — Linux PrivEsc / Windows PrivEsc**
- Free on YouTube: search "TCM Security Linux Privilege Escalation"
- Watch these before attempting Medium boxes

## Curated HTB Machine Lists

**TJ_Null OSCP-like list**: github.com/tjnull/OSCP-Stuff
- Retired HTB machines with similar style to OSCP exam
- Work through this list in order after finishing TryHackMe Jr path

## Video Walkthroughs After Solving

**IppSec**: youtube.com/@ippsec
- Detailed walkthroughs of retired HTB machines
- Watch only AFTER you've solved (or fully attempted) the same box
- Compare your approach — IppSec often shows 2-3 different paths to root

## How to Use Videos Without Wasting Time

1. Do the lab machine first — even if you don't finish it
2. Watch the walkthrough after
3. Note every technique you didn't know
4. Replicate that technique on your own VM

Don't watch walkthroughs for boxes you haven't attempted. You'll recognize the answer when you see it during the exam and won't know how to execute it.

## Course vs Lab Hours Ratio

Aim for at least 3 hours of lab work for every 1 hour of video. If you're watching more than you're hacking, rebalance.

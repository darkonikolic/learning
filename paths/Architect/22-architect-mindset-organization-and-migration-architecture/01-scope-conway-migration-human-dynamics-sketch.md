# Unit 1 — Scope: organisational dynamics & migration

Architecture changes fail when incentives and communication fail—not only when diagrams are wrong.

---

## Architectural outcomes

Align service boundaries with **Conway realities** instead of pretending org charts do not shape systems.
Sketch phased **migration** strategies that shrink blast radius and avoid big-bang cutovers by default.
Name **stress, ethics, handover hygiene** explicitly: turnover, burnout, and unclear ownership degrade architecture faster than naive designs.

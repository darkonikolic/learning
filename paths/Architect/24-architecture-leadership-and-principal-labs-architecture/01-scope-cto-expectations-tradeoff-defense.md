# Unit 1 — Scope: principal-ready posture

Architect leaders defend decisions under uncertainty—they do not memorize buzzword bingo.

---

## Architectural outcomes

- Practise **executive succinctness**: risk, cost horizon, reversible versus irreversible decisions.
- Name **ethical clarity**: cut through vendor hype with evidence and explicit assumptions.
- Run **architecture reviews** so dissent surfaces early—not as rubber stamps.

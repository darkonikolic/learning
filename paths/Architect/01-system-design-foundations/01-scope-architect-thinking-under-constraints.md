# Unit 1 — Scope: architects decide under constraints

> **Suggested cadence (informational):** prefixes are topic order—not a timetable. Pace by depth, not by calendar myths.

## What an architect optimises

An architect is not “the cleverest programmer.” They make **technical decisions under constraints**:

```
budget • schedule • team skills • latency • scale • ops • security • compliance • maintainability • risk
```

Thinking shift to internalise:

- From “make it run” toward **years** of viable ownership (“how it lives for ~3 years”).
- From “modernest stack” toward **least-bad compromise** (`tradeoff`).
- From “anything is possible” toward **articulated boundaries**.

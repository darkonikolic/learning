# Unit 1 — Scope: cloud as failure domains & economic topology

## Architectural outcomes

Articulate—not AWS trivia flashcards—

- Failure domains spanning **AZ versus region**.
- Stateless elasticity versus unavoidable **state placement** honesty.
- **CDN** and edge interplay with **cache invalidation** (ties back to **`06-*`** areas where you studied edge/advanced caching patterns).
- **Networking boundaries**: private versus public subnets and where security controls anchor (forward link to **`13-*`** for deeper threat/boundary drills).
- **Managed services taxation** versus autonomy (for example RDS versus self-hosted operational burden).
- **Backup and DR vocabulary**: RPO/RTO framing without pretending every workload needs synchronous multi-region redundancy.
- **Cost posture visibility**: forecasting, mis-sizing risk, waste—without spreadsheet theatre masking unknown demand drivers.

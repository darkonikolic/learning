# Unit 1 — Scope: aligning architecture narratives

Architects synthesise viewpoints for humans—engineers, product, executives—without losing technical integrity.

---

## Architectural outcomes

Practise **C4 overlays** tailoring abstraction depth per stakeholder honestly.
Maintain **architecture decision records (ADR)** structure capturing context, alternatives, consequences.
Conduct **risk / trade-off framing** bridging uncertainty without false precision.
Articulate roadmap tension: pragmatic incremental delivery versus ideal target states.
